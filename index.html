About

Game developer,
modder & coder

I'm a game developer. I started writing code in 2016, and most of my public work so far is in game modding, where I've taken projects from first prototype to public release: code, content integration and final packaging.

Today I build full games in Unreal Engine (Blueprints) and GameMaker, with a focus on gameplay systems, interface, audio and getting a project to a finished, playable state. I also take on commissions for custom mods and game-related coding.
